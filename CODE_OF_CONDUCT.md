# Code of conduct

Help make Impossible Print a considerate place to learn, build and review work.

Treat people respectfully. Discuss specific behaviour and evidence, explain disagreements clearly, and give others room to ask beginner questions. Welcome different backgrounds, abilities and levels of experience.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention and deliberate disruption are unacceptable. Do not share another person's private information or artwork without permission.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful contributions, close disruptive discussions and limit participation when necessary. Enforcement should be proportionate to the behaviour and protect the people affected.

To report a concern, ask the maintainer for a private contact route without posting sensitive details in public. Security vulnerabilities follow the separate [security policy](SECURITY.md). This policy applies to the project's repositories and project-managed community spaces.
